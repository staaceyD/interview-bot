Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Interview Bot is a free practice tool for software engineering interview questions, run by Anastasiia Derevianko as a personal project. This page says what it keeps about you, why, and who else sees it.

What is kept, and where

  • Your sign-in. When you sign in with Google or GitHub, the app receives your account ID, your name, and your email address if the provider has verified it. It keeps them in a signed cookie in your browser for up to 14 days, or until you sign out. No password, and no access token from Google or GitHub, is stored.
  • Your interviews. The topics and difficulty you pick, the questions you are asked, the grades you get and any worked answers, stored in a database together with your account ID, so only you can open them.
  • A daily count of the questions each account has asked, to enforce the daily limit.
  • In your browser only: the ID of the interview you are on, so a refresh picks it up again, and your notes, which never leave your browser.

The text of your answers is sent to be graded and is not stored; only the grade is. Your name and email are not stored in the database.

Who else sees it

  • Google or GitHub, whichever you sign in with, under their own privacy policies.
  • Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the questions and grades your answers. The questions and your answers are sent to it for that purpose.
  • Render, which hosts the app and keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times.
  • Neon, which hosts the database.

Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else. The app has no analytics and no tracking.

Why

Everything above is used only to run the app: to know who you are, keep your interviews yours, grade your answers, and keep the free daily limit fair.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time, which removes the cookie. To have your interviews and daily counts deleted, email aderevianko@proton.me from the address you signed in with, and they will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. The current version is always on this page.
